# NX-10: Joint-level interpolation (B3)

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Jerk-limited, clock-synchronised profiles
- [x] Late and missing goals handled
- [x] H2 measured (partly met, see log)

## Tests

- `crates/nexus-node/src/reference.rs`: quintic hits both end states; goals reached on
  their due cycle and a smooth stop when they stop; two joints reach a shared due cycle
  together although one received its goal 60 ms later
- `sim/src/harness/interpolation.test.ts`: figure of eight and pick and place, jerk at
  least 2.5× lower with lower path error; one goal frame per joint per policy step

## Log

- 2026-10-21: `SplineInterpolator` turns every goal into a quintic from the current
  reference (position, velocity, acceleration) to the goal's position and velocity at its
  due bus cycle. Goals due sooner than 10 ms are pushed back; when the next goal is late
  or lost the joint coasts to a stop over 150 ms. `SwitchableInterpolator` holds the
  setpoint follower and the spline and switches on `Settings.interpolation` without a
  jump.

**H2 results (KX-6 at home pose, 15 Hz policy with 30 ms latency, 15 ms jitter, 5 %
drops; central host loop 250 Hz with 10 % late ticks; seed 7):**

| Task | Jerk RMS, central → joint-level | Ratio | Path error RMS (max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figure of eight | 861 → 286 m/s³ | 3.0× | 7.7 (31.6) → 2.4 (13.1) mm |
| Pick and place | 961 → 191 m/s³ | 5.0× | 6.6 (30.1) → 1.6 (5.7) mm |
| Fast reach | 1145 → 822 m/s³ | 1.4× | 39.5 (186.5) → 22.9 (97.1) mm |

Target was jerk ≥ 5× lower at equal or better path error. Path error is better in every
task. The jerk target is met for pick and place and missed for the figure of eight and
the fast reach, where the arm's own 4 Hz servo bandwidth, not interpolation, limits how
smooth the tool can be. Host work: central sent 10 122 setpoints for 117 IK solves,
joint-level sent 702 goals for the same solves (H8 groundwork).

- 2026-10-21 (correction): a bug in the policy emulator gave the last two steps of a task
  the same target time when the task length was a multiple of the policy period. The
  goal velocity was then divided by almost zero, and the arm lurched at the end of the
  task in both interpolation modes. Fixed at the source (no duplicate-time steps) and in
  the consumer (steps less than 1 ms apart are ignored). The figure of eight also gets a
  ramp-out, so it ends at rest like the other tasks. Re-measured H2 (same settings, seed 7):

| Task | Jerk RMS, central → joint-level | Ratio | Path error RMS (max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figure of eight | 815 → 261 m/s³ | 3.1× | 7.2 (31.6) → 2.0 (11.6) mm |
| Pick and place | 961 → 191 m/s³ | 5.0× | 6.6 (30.1) → 1.6 (5.7) mm |
| Fast reach | 1145 → 822 m/s³ | 1.4× | 39.5 (186.5) → 22.9 (97.1) mm |

  Conclusions unchanged: path error is better in every task, and the 5× jerk target is
  met for pick and place only.
