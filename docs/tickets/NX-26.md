# NX-26: Budget and premium actuator grades

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.
Asked for by the owner: how does the system behave on a budget actuator with strong
cogging (a low-cost QDD module) against a high-quality one?

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Actuator grades in the sim, each a set of drawn imperfections around the
  datasheet: premium (low cogging and ripple, tight current sensing) and budget (strong
  cogging, phase-current offset and gain mismatch, more friction and gear play)
- [ ] Phase-current sensing errors are modelled where they act: per-phase offset gives
  torque ripple at the electrical frequency, gain mismatch at twice it, and the joint
  measures the current it thinks it drives, not the true one
- [ ] The phase current the joint measures is part of its state, available to its own
  models (PHNN residual, report to source)
- [ ] A per-grade proof: tap settling, task tracking and vibration, report to source,
  with and without each joint's learned model; reported per grade in RESULTS.md
- [ ] Demo: actuator grade selectable

## Tests

- Unit tests: offset gives the 1× electrical ripple, gain mismatch the 2×, at the
  expected amplitude
- Grades drawn deterministically per seed
