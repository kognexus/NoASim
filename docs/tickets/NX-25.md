# NX-25: Network damping without extra vibration under a policy computer

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.
Found in NX-24: with the VLA computer (15 Hz goals), network damping made every joint's
housing vibrate 10–20 % more during a task than joint control alone. With the classic
controller it made no difference.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The cause is measured, not guessed, and written down
- [x] With the VLA computer, a network-damped arm's joints vibrate no more than with
  joint control only during pick and place and the figure of eight (KX-6L and KX-6,
  per joint, within 5 %)
- [x] Tracking during tasks no worse than joint control only (NX-23 holds)
- [x] Tap settling (H1) and the classic-controller numbers unchanged within 5 %
- [x] `npm run prove` re-run; RESULTS.md and limitations updated

## Tests

- A harness test: under the VLA computer, network damping does not raise task vibration
- Rust and TypeScript suites passing

## Log

- 2026-10-21: **Reproduced** (KX-6L and KX-6, figure of eight and pick and place, VLA
  computer, the demo's vibration meter, s 1–8): network damping 2–10 % above joint
  control only on joints 2–6, tracking unchanged.
- **Where the extra is.** Band powers of J5's housing rate (KX-6L, figure of eight, °/s
  RMS):

| Band, Hz | 3–5 | 5–8 | 8–12 | 12–18 | 18–25 | 25–35 | 35–60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Joint control only | 1.52 | 3.28 | 3.77 | 5.83 | 1.92 | 3.82 | 1.75 |
| Joint-local | 1.46 | 1.89 | 3.54 | 6.31 | 2.01 | 4.27 | 1.91 |
| Network | 1.40 | 2.05 | 3.99 | 6.81 | 2.32 | 4.54 | 2.02 |

  Damping takes 40 % off the arm's ring (5–8 Hz), and adds 10–20 % at the goal rate
  (15 Hz) and its second harmonic. The cause: damping acts on the measured rate minus
  the joint's reference velocity (NX-23), and the reference has kinks at every new goal,
  where the interpolator joins one segment to the next. Damping on the difference pulls
  the joint onto those kinks, so a damped joint follows them more tightly than an
  undamped one.
- **Fix.** The reference velocity is smoothed before it is subtracted, at half the rate
  goals arrive at: a stream of goals carries no plan above that. Each joint measures the
  gap between the goals it receives (`GoalRate` in `reference.rs`, an average that skips
  pauses between tasks); `ReferenceSmoother` in `damping.rs` low-passes at half that
  rate, and passes the reference through before two goals have arrived or when the cutoff
  is above a quarter of the cycle rate. A fixed cutoff (3, 4, 6 and 10 Hz tried) fixed
  the VLA case too, but lagged the classic controller's smooth setpoints, so damping
  braked them again: KX-6 pick tracking 0.63 → 0.89 mm at 4 Hz.
- **Result** (joints 1–6, ratio to joint control only; tracking RMS):

| Arm, computer, task | Network before | Network after | Tracking, none / after |
|---|---|---|---|
| KX-6L, VLA, figure of eight | 1.00 1.04 1.08 1.08 1.10 1.09 | 1.00 0.87 0.90 0.89 0.90 0.91 | 1.57 / 1.45 mm |
| KX-6L, VLA, pick and place | 1.00 1.02 1.02 1.05 1.10 1.08 | 1.00 0.90 0.91 0.91 0.99 0.98 | 2.55 / 2.42 mm |
| KX-6, VLA, figure of eight | 1.00 1.05 1.08 1.04 1.06 1.05 | 1.00 0.90 0.93 0.91 0.87 0.89 | 1.47 / 1.37 mm |

  Under the VLA computer network damping now takes 1–13 % off every moving joint's
  vibration, and tracks better than no damping. Classic-controller numbers are the same
  as before to within 1 %. Tap settling at rest is unchanged to the last digit (KX-6L:
  0.866 mm mean distance over 1 s; KX-6: 0.673 mm), because at rest the reference is
  zero.
- **Found on the way, not fixed:** joint-local damping under the classic controller
  already made the outer joints vibrate 6–13 % more on pick and place (J5, J6) and
  tracked 0.1–0.2 mm worse, before this change and after it. Recorded in
  `docs/limitations.md` with its likely cause. The network modes do not show it.
- **Proof re-run** (`npm run prove`, 17 min): every verdict as before; H1 to H9 numbers
  unchanged. H10b's task tracking moved by −0.2 to +0.15 mm for every model alike,
  including the datasheet-actuator reference (figure of eight 2.44 → 2.52 mm on
  kx6-s300), so the model comparison stands; slow-sweep deviations within 0.003 mrad.
- **Test:** `web/src/rig.test.ts`: under 15 Hz policy goals, network damping keeps every
  moving joint at least 3 % below joint control only (fails on the old code: 54.1
  against 51.9 °/s). Rust: `GoalRate` averages gaps and skips pauses. The demo's
  vibration meter moved to `sim/src/analysis/vibration.ts`, so experiments can use it.
