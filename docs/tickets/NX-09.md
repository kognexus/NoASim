# NX-09: Host / VLA emulator + central baseline

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Scripted tasks (pick and place, figure eight, fast reach) at 10 to 50 Hz with jitter and dropouts
- [x] DLS IK
- [x] Central interpolation baseline
- [x] Host compute counter for H8

## Tests

- `sim/src/host/host.test.ts`: IK reaches targets within 10 cm of home to < 0.1 mm;
  every task starts at home and moves below 2 m/s; the policy emulator is late by its
  latency plus jitter and drops the configured share of steps
- `sim/src/harness/motion.test.ts`: central baseline follows a figure of eight (path
  error RMS < 15 mm) and counts IK solves and streamed setpoints

## Log

- 2026-10-21: `VlaEmulator` emits a target one policy step ahead at 10–50 Hz (default
  15 Hz), 30 ms late plus up to 15 ms of jitter, and drops 5 % of steps. Tasks:
  figure of eight, pick and place, fast reach, around `KX6_HOME` (tool pointing down at
  (0.60, 0, 0.25) m). The stretched start pose is a reach singularity, so it stays the
  vibration test pose only.
- Host IK: damped least squares on the nominal kinematics with orientation held,
  warm-started from the previous solution.
- Central baseline (`CentralInterpolation`): IK per policy step, linear joint-space
  interpolation on a 250 Hz host loop that runs 1–4 periods late 10 % of the time,
  setpoints streamed to all six joints each tick. Joints follow each setpoint with its
  velocity (`StepInterpolator`).
- Joint-level goals (`JointLevelGoals`): one goal per policy step per joint, stamped
  with the bus cycle it is due. NX-10 adds the node-side interpolator.
- `HostLoad` counts IK solves, interpolation steps and goal frames (H8). First central
  figure of eight: jerk RMS 861 m/s³, path error RMS 7.7 mm (max 31.6 mm), 117 IK
  solves and 10 122 streamed setpoints in 8.5 s.
