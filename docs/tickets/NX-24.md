# NX-24: Demo for comparison

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.
Asked for by the owner after trying the demo.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Orbiting, zooming or panning either 3D view moves both
- [x] Each arm's charts sit under its own view, and each kind of chart shares one scale
  across the two arms
- [x] A chart of the vibration each joint feels
- [x] A "?" with a tooltip on every control group and chart

## Tests

- `web/src/vibration.test.ts`: the vibration meter ignores steady rotation and reads a
  10 Hz shake as its RMS (within 10 %)
- `web/src/rig.test.ts` unchanged and passing
- Checked in the browser pane: both views turn together, a tap shows on both arms'
  charts at one scale, tooltips stay on screen

## Log

- 2026-10-21: **Shared camera.** The right view is built with the left one's camera and
  orbit target (`ArmView(canvas, plant, shareWith)`), so both always show the arms from
  the same angle.
- **Per-arm charts.** Under each arm: tool distance from target (labelled with the arm's
  damping mode) and the vibration each joint feels, one line per joint. The two charts of
  each kind are drawn on the larger of their two scales, so heights compare directly.
- **Vibration per joint** (`web/src/vibration.ts`): what each joint reports on the bus, its
  housing IMU's rotation rate, high-passed at 3 Hz so commanded motion drops out, as an
  RMS over 50 ms, in °/s. At rest it shows the gyros' noise floor (about 0.3°/s).
- **Help.** A "?" next to every control group, the arm bars and the charts; hover or
  keyboard focus shows what the control does. Tips open toward the side of the screen
  with room.
- Also landing in this commit, though they belong to the tickets named: the "Driven by"
  selector (NX-22) and the chart legend naming each arm's mode (NX-23), which share
  files with this work.
