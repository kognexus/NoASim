## Log

- 2026-10-21: **Inference in the node** (`crates/nexus-node/src/phnn.rs`).
  - `PhnnModel` reads a flat block of `f32` words: format version, joint, constants,
    cogging orders and weights, then three tanh MLPs (gear, viscous, Coulomb) and the
    gain's networks. The gear spring's torque needs the derivative of its network, which
    is computed analytically (back through both tanh layers).
  - The block is refused if it is short, of another version or joint, not finite, or has
    a non-positive scale.
  - Settings `model = 2` selects it (`ModelChoice::Learned`); without a loaded model the
    joint falls back to the datasheet.
  - WebAssembly: the I/O block gained a model area, with `nexus_load_model` to install it
    and a diagnostic `nexus_model_residual`. `packPhnnModel` (TS) packs an exported JSON in
    the same order, and `ArmSystem` loads each joint's model at power-up (`models`).
- **Used for:**
  - feed-forward: the command that cancels friction and cogging, `(R·ω − cogging) / g`.
    It is evaluated at the measured motor angle; at the reference output angle the twist
    misphased the order-864 cogging terms;
  - the self-test residual of report-to-source (`DynamicsModel::unexplained` with a
    `MotorBalance`). The datasheet model keeps the old residual as its default.
- **Not used for the damping law's energy.** The plan listed it, but the damping law and
  its energy tank stay model-free on purpose (B5): a learned model is never in the safety
  path.
- **Fixes to the self-test found here** (re-measured below):
  - The residual used the commands sent at k−3 and k−4; the cycles around k−1 felt those
    sent at k−2 and k−3, as the trainer's alignment says. Corrected.
  - On the lightweight KX-6L a joint never claimed its own ripple. The coherence test
    compared the order's amplitude with the residual's whole RMS, which link vibration
    far from the order dominates. Each order now has a lock-in reference: the same
    demodulation 3 Hz above the order's frequency, which measures the noise right next
    to it. A claim needs the order to stand 3× above it.
    - A first try at 1.13× the order failed. At the mesh order (≈ 7 Hz) that is under 1 Hz
      away, the 0.5 Hz demodulators let a third of the ripple into the reference, and H7
      dropped to 6/10 found.
    - Felt reports, which only corroborate, are made when either detector sees the order:
      coherence ≥ 0.5 or 1.5× above the reference. Coherence misses on the lightweight
      arm; the reference misses next to the stiff arm's modes.
    - The NX-12 tests and the demo's tests pass on both arms.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  **Met as worded** (never unstable; the network term never put energy in). Not without

  degrades performance; the tank and the servo keep it bounded.
