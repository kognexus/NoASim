# NX-23: Damping leaves commanded motion alone

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.
Found by the owner in the demo: during tasks, the tool-distance chart showed the
network-damped arm further from its target than the undamped one.

- [x] Tap settling at rest unchanged
- [x] The demo chart has a legend naming each arm's mode
- [x] Results re-measured

## Log

- 2026-10-21: **The finding.** In the demo, during a task, the left arm (network damping)
  strayed further from its target than the right (joint control only). The chart
  had no legend, so it looked swapped. It wasn't: measured in the demo's own rig on the
  KX-6L, tool distance from the path, RMS (max):

| Task | Joint control only | Joint-local | Network |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figure of eight | 1.44 (11.65) mm | 1.80 (11.39) mm | 2.32 (11.54) mm |
| Pick and place | 3.79 (36.0) mm | 3.72 (33.4) mm | 4.15 (31.5) mm |

- **Cause.** Both damping laws damped the joint's *absolute* rate (local) or the link tip's
  rate relative to the housing (network), high-passed at 1 Hz. During a commanded move,
  part of that rate is the move itself. The damping braked it, and because of the
  high-pass it kept pulling for a while after the move ended: the long tails in the
  chart. No proof scenario showed this; taps happen at rest, and the task tests (H2) run
  without damping.
- **Fix** (`damping.rs`): the damping context carries the joint's own reference velocity,
  and both signals subtract it. For the network term this removes the commanded part
  exactly: the tip's rate relative to the joint's own housing is the joint's rate plus
  its link's bending, and the commanded part of the joint's rate is its reference
  velocity. Joint-local damping still sees the housing's commanded rotation (the inner
  joints' moves); only its own share is removed. The relayed second-hop rate (weight 0
  in the tuned gains) still contains the next joint's move.
- **After** (same rig and tasks):

| Task | Joint control only | Joint-local | Network |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figure of eight | 1.44 (11.65) mm | 1.43 (11.61) mm | 1.45 (11.20) mm |
| Pick and place | 3.79 (36.0) mm | 3.63 (35.8) mm | 3.66 (35.7) mm |

  Tap settling at rest is identical to the last digit, since the reference velocity is
  zero there, so the NX-11 tuning still holds.
- **Results re-measured** (`npm run prove`, `docs/RESULTS.md`): every verdict unchanged.
  H1, H6 and the other tap-at-rest numbers are identical. Two numbers moved because their
  scenarios run network damping while the arm moves:
  - H7 on hidden actuators, datasheet residual: 20/30 right, 9 false claims (was 23 and
    6); the learned residual is unchanged at 29/30 with 1;
  - H10b slow-sweep deviation: learned 0.108, datasheet 0.190, RLS 1.678 mrad (was 0.111,
    0.193, 1.680).
- **Demo:** the tool-distance chart now has a legend that names each arm by its damping
  mode and follows the mode selectors. It lands with NX-24, which changes the same files.
