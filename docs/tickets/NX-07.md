# NX-07: Local control + flex estimation (B4, mode 2)

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] ~~Cascaded loop on the motor side~~ → output-encoder PD with rotor damping (ADR-0003)
- [x] Nominal and RLS `DynamicsModel`s
- [x] Gear twist checked against plant truth; link-tip rate (gear + link flex) estimated from the neighbour IMU in `NetworkDamper` (published and checked in NX-11)
- [ ] Joint-local damping beats network off: **not met under a fair jitter budget** (see log); revisited in NX-11

## Tests

- `crates/nexus-node/src/dynamics.rs`: RLS recovers textbook friction from the torque
  balance
- `crates/nexus-node/src/damping.rs`: energy tank never releases more than it took
- `crates/nexus-node/src/filters.rs`: low-pass settles to DC, high-pass removes it
- `sim/src/harness/control.test.ts`: gear twist from the dual encoders within 1.5e-4 rad

- `sim/src/analysis/metrics.test.ts`, `sim/src/plant/kinematics.test.ts`

## Log

- 2026-10-21: Node control stack: `JointController` (output-encoder PD, integral,
  rotor-against-gear damping), estimator with filtered motor acceleration, gear torque
  from the twist (no torque sensor) and motor torque from current. `ModelSet` carries
  the nominal model and an RLS friction fit (Kalman form; exponential forgetting lost
  positive definiteness in f32). `LocalDamper` (own IMU + output encoder) and
  `NetworkDamper` (link-tip rate from both IMUs; falls back to local when neighbour data
  is older than 8 cycles), each behind an `EnergyTank`. A run-time `Settings` frame
  switches damping mode and model.
- Harness: 1-cycle actuation delay (EtherCAT DC), optional physics substeps, a
  `configure` hook, gain sizing from nominal kinematics (`harness/gains.ts`), the tap
  scenario and settling/RMS metrics.
- Findings and the baseline decision are in ADR-0003. In short: QDD rotor inertia caps
  motor-side damping, adjacent joints across a flexible link cap joint damping through
  observer lag, and the observer trades lag against noise. On the stiff KX-6 the links
  barely bend, so network damping has little to do. On a lightweight variant with
  flexible links it is decisive (ringing that never settles, against about 0.6 s).
- **Acceptance criterion not met:** joint-local damping beat the baseline only by
  exceeding the jitter budget (settling 0.40 s against 0.72 s, but 0.6 mm jitter against
  0.08 mm). Following the working agreement (tune, then report), the criterion stays
  open and is measured properly in NX-11 with a fair tuner on both arms.
- Integral action is off until NX-08 adds gravity feed-forward; the start-up test allows
  12 cm of sag until then.
