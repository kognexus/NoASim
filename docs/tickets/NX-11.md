# NX-11: Neighbour damping + passivity guard (B1a, B5)

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Each joint as a port-Hamiltonian subsystem
- [x] Neighbour exchange as power ports
- [x] Network term as pure damping injection
- [x] Energy tank on measured power with a delay bound
- [x] Posture-aware authority sharing
- [x] Torque cap
- [x] Stale-data fallback
- [x] ADR-0004 gives the stability argument
- [x] Lightweight arm KX-6L (flexible links, first locked mode ≈ 5 Hz) next to the stiff KX-6
- [x] Fair per-mode tuner: fastest settling under one rest-jitter budget, several seeds
- [x] Joint-local vs off revisited (NX-07's open criterion)
- [x] H1 and H6 measured on both arms

## Tests

- `sim/src/harness/network.test.ts` (seed 31, never used by the tuner): on KX-6L the
  network settles a tap more than 2× faster than joint control alone and faster than
  joint-local damping, quiet and within the jitter budget (H1); with 10 cycles of latency
  and 20 % lost frames the damping work stays below the tank capacity and the arm stays
  quiet (H6); cutting the bus behind joint 3 falls back cleanly; joint 2 relays joint 3's
  gyro
- `crates/nexus-node/src/damping.rs`: energy tank never releases more than it took
- `sim/src/plant/plant.test.ts`: KX-6L first mode 4–8 Hz, < 2 % damping
- `experiments/src/tune.ts`, `experiments/src/robustness.ts` (the numbers below)

## Log

- 2026-10-21: **KX-6L**, the same joints on lightweight flexible links (60 % mass, 10 %
  bending stiffness): first locked modes 6.2 Hz (1.2 %) vertical and 6.7 Hz (1.4 %)
  lateral. KX-6 stays as the stiff reference.
- **Network law** (`NetworkDamper`): each joint damps the rate of its link tip relative to
  its own housing, measured by the outer neighbour's gyro (no differentiation, includes
  link bending), plus optionally the rate one link further out, which every node now
  relays in its state frame (`outer_rate`). The gain scales with the current outboard
  inertia from the gravity chain (posture-aware sharing, clamped 0.25–2×). Signals are
  high-passed at 1 Hz and low-passed (2nd order) at 15 Hz. Without that low-pass the
  network loop drove a 120 Hz clamped-link mode, which the tank bounded but could not
  quieten (ADR-0004). Energy tank 0.05 J per joint, torque cap from the host limits, and
  neighbour data older than 8 cycles drops the term.
- Plan wording: the "port-Hamiltonian subsystem / power ports" criteria are met in the
  form of the passivity argument in ADR-0004 (collocated joint loops, a tank on measured
  port power). No explicit port-Hamiltonian state-space model was built; the PHNN
  tickets (NX-13–15) add one per joint.
- **Fair tuner** (`experiments/src/tune.ts`): the same joint controller in every mode.
  Each mode's damping gains are chosen for the fastest mean settling over 2 seeds × 2 tap
  directions, subject to rest jitter ≤ 0.1 mm and quiet housings (≤ 1.05 g RMS).
  Results (`sim/src/harness/tuned.ts`):

| Arm | Off | Joint-local | Network | Network ÷ off | Network ÷ local |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KX-6L | 0.924 s (jitter 0.015 mm) | 0.429 s (0.089 mm) | 0.336 s (0.015 mm) | 2.75× | 1.28× |
| KX-6 | 0.803 s (0.021 mm) | 0.314 s (0.084 mm) | 0.280 s (0.016 mm) | 2.87× | 1.12× |

  H1's targets (≥ 10× vs off, ≥ 3× vs joints alone) are **not met**. The targets assumed
  an arm whose structural modes are almost undamped (ζ ≈ 1–3 %). A tuned joint servo
  already damps the dominant modes well, so there is much less left to gain. What the
  network does buy, beyond faster settling: the same speed as joint-local damping at a
  sixth of its rest jitter, because a gyro needs no differentiation. NX-07's open
  criterion "joint-local beats off" is met under the fair budget (2.2× on KX-6L,
  2.6× on KX-6). NX-19 re-measures everything over more seeds.

  H6 met: the damping work never rose above 0.3 mJ (tank capacity 50 mJ), the housings
  stayed quiet, and nothing became unstable. Beyond 2 ms of latency the network term
  switches itself off by design and the arm behaves exactly like joint control alone.
  Frame loss first exposed two bugs, now fixed: discovery and settings frames were sent
  once and could be lost (nodes stayed unconfigured). Discovery now retries, and the host
  re-sends its settings every 100 ms.
- Also fixed: a node that loses its neighbour now keeps the last known gravity chain
  instead of dropping the outboard load.
- Landed early in this commit: the impact, heartbeat, acknowledge and fault frames, plus
  the node's impact detector and supervisor (disarmed unless the host arms them). Their
  behaviour and tests belong to NX-16/17.
