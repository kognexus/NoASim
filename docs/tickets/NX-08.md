# NX-08: Distributed gravity compensation + payload estimate (B10)

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Per-link gravity direction from the accelerometer, fused with the gyro
- [x] Tip-inward pass of mass and centre of mass over the bus (`Chain` frame, 1 kHz)
- [x] Gravity feed-forward
- [x] Payload estimate from holding torque (gear-twist torque, best lever flows outward)
- [x] Base-tilt and moving-base injectors (gravity vector, by equivalence)
- [x] H9 measured (partly met, see log)

## Tests

- `crates/nexus-node/src/gravity.rs`: holding torque and payload lever of a horizontal
  link; up estimate follows a tilt and ignores a short shake
- `crates/nexus-node/src/vector.rs`: Rodrigues rotation
- `sim/src/harness/gravity.test.ts`: shoulder and elbow feed-forward within 1 % of rated;
  payload found within ±10 %; 15° sideways tilt: tool within 1 mm and at least 5× better
  than the level-base model; moving base: at least 2× better; still from power-on
- Protocol golden bytes extended with `Chain` and the 8-byte `Settings`

## Log

- 2026-10-21: Each node estimates "up" in its housing frame (accelerometer fused with
  gyro, 20 ms correction), sums the load beyond it from its outer neighbour's `Chain`
  frame plus its own link, and adds the holding torque as feed-forward (smoothed at
  15 Hz). The best payload estimate (largest lever) flows outward to the tip node. A
  `Settings.gravity` switch selects off / own IMU / encoders from a level base (the
  comparison model, also distributed).
- Found and fixed: MuJoCo reports zero acceleration on bodies welded to the world, so the
  base joint's IMU read no gravity. The plant now gives static-body accelerometers the
  specific force of gravity.
- Found: at damping ratio 0.7 the rotor–gear modes rang (≈ 400 m/s² at the housings,
  invisible at the tool). The baseline moves to ζ 0.3 / 150 Hz (ADR-0003 update).
- Found: tilting the base *within* the arm's plane only scales gravity, and the payload
  estimator absorbs that as a lighter payload. The tilt test therefore tilts sideways.
