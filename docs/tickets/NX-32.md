# NX-32: A colleague's frame-aligned damping law, tested without a tank

See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) for the behaviours (B*) and hypotheses (H*) referenced here.
A colleague's Lyapunov derivation (`lyapunov_qdd.pdf`, shared by the owner) argues that
network damping on frame-aligned IMU rates drains energy unconditionally:

```text
Δω_ij = ω_i − R_ij(q)·ω_j          (j's gyro resolved in i's frame by the encoders)
τ_i   = −d_i·θ̇_m,i − Σ_{j∈N(i)} D_ij · HPF(Δω_ij)
V̇     = −q̇ᵀ D_local q̇ − Δωᵀ L Δω ≤ 0
```

ADR-0004 holds that a joint's torque on a rate measured across its link is not
collocated, so its power has no fixed sign, and that the energy tank is what bounds it.
The owner asked to settle this by running the law as written.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The law as written, outside the joints, on the simulator's gyros resolved in each
  joint's frame by its encoder angles, high-passed, both neighbours, no energy tank
- [ ] Swept over gain (a quarter to eight times the network gain) and bus delay (0, 2,
  4 cycles), both arms: the energy each joint's term puts in, tap settling, stability
- [ ] The no-delay case on the linearised arm: whether the closed loop is stable
- [ ] Recorded in ADR-0004 whichever way it falls
